# Week 1 | Timetable design and weekly records

We mapped the path from a class meeting to a weekly report. Students need to keep a teacher's exact assignment instructions and exam scope beside the class. Research students also need records for meetings that have no corresponding timetable entry.

We chose dated CSV rows for the first importer because the school timetable could be viewed or captured as an image. Each meeting has a stable source key. A later correction to the room or time should update that meeting and keep its notes attached.

Our deliverables were the initial data model, the input contract and sample files covering normal rows, corrections and invalid data. Week 2 used these examples to check the implementation.
