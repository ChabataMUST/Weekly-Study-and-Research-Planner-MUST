# Weekly-Study-and-Research-Planner
We propose the Weekly Study and Research Planner for students at Macau University of
Science and Technology (MUST) who manage coursework and, in some cases, research.
In interviews, four undergraduate and three postgraduate students described difficulty
reviewing several deadlines together, keeping instructions given in class and finding
earlier notes. WeMust shows class times and locations, while Moodle provides course
materials and deadlines. Students still need to open individual entries to read their
requirements and keep personal notes elsewhere. Our application will import a
timetable, connect dated notes to courses, assignments, examinations and projects, and
turn those notes into tasks. It will suggest work periods within the student's available
time and prepare an editable weekly report for PDF export. Postgraduates can combine
taught courses with meetings, reading and experiments; students without taught courses
can use calendar weeks. Students at other institutions could use the same functions with
a supported timetable. We have made automatic import and weekly PDF export
mandatory for our release. The two-person team will first establish an import route and
produce a sample PDF, then develop and integrate the remaining features over ten
project weeks. ZhaoYue will coordinate the work and own timetable and task features.
WangYule will own requirement records, retrieval, research records and reports. Both
members will contribute to every activity and check each other's work. Tests will cover
import accuracy, retained history, retrieval, scheduling, report content, privacy and the
effort needed for routine tasks. The delivery will include the web application, source
code, setup guidance, sample data and design documents.
